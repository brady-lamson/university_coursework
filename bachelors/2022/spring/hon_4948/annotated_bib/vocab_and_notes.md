# Vocabulary

## Words I've learned
- **Photovoltaic**: Otherwise known as *PV* or a *solar cell*, is a nonmechanical device that converts sunlight directly into electricity. Some PV cells can convert artifical light into electricity. These are made of *semiconductor* material. 

- **Semiconductor:** Any material that has a limited capacity for conducting an electric current. 

	- Certain semiconductors, including silicon, gallium arsenide, copper indium diselenide, and cadmium telluride, are uniquely suited to the photovoltaic conversion process.

- **Photon:** Particles of solar energy. The energy these carry varies depending on the different wavelengths of the *solar spectrum*. 

- **Solar Spectrum:** The total distribution of electromagnetic radiation emanating from the sun. 

- **Photovoltaic Module:** It is a framed unit made up of many solar panels that are made up of many photovoltaic cells. Otherwise known as a *solar module*. 

- **Photovoltaic Array:**  An array comprised of two or hundreds of PV modules. 

- **NWP Models:** Numerical weather prediction models.

## Photovoltaics and Electricity
- When photons strike a PV cell, they may reflect off the cell, pass through the cell, or be absorbed by the semiconductor material. Only the absorbed photons provide energy to generate electricity. [Source](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/solar/photovoltaics-and-electricity.php)
