Academic Questions:

	- 1) What is your academic discipline? 


	- 2) How would you describe the history of {your discipline}? What are some major ways that have caused it to change over time?
	

	- 3) How would you describe the different methods of inquiry in {this discipline}?
	
		- a) Many disciplines use statistics as a method of inquiry, how often do you pull on methods of inquiry outside of statistics to help further your research? 
	
	- 4) What current and/or long-standing discussions and debates are important to {the discipline}?
		- a) How do these debates shape the research in {the discipline}? 
		- b) How do you see {this discipline} changing in the next 10 years? 
		
	- 5) What excites you about {your discipline}, 
		- a) frustrates you?
	
	- 6) What did you wish you knew before getting into {your discipline}?

	- 6) If you had the chance to freely pivot to {another discipline}, which one would you be most interested in?
