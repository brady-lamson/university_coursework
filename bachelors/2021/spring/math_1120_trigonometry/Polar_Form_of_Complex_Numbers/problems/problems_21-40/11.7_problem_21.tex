\begin{problem}[21.]
    $z = 6 cis(0)$
\end{problem}

\begin{solution}
    So, based on the format of the polar representation we know a few things. The first number, $6$, is our radius and the second number, $0$, is our $\theta$. With this knowledge we can start to work backwards. 
    
    The big thing we need to do is rewrite "cis" as "cos" and "sin".
    
    \begin{align}
        z = 6 cis(0) &= |z| (\cos(\theta) + i \sin(\theta)) \\
        &= 6 \cdot (\cos(0) + i \sin(0)) \\
        &= 6 + i \cdot 0
    \end{align}
    \[\boxed{Re(z) = 6} \; \text{and} \; \boxed{Im(z) = 0}\]
    
    From this we can rewrite our equation in rectangular form. 
    
    \[\boxed{z = 6}\]
\end{solution}
