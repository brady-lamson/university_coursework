\textbf{Problem 24.} \\
\hrule 

An insurance company offers its policyholders a number of different premium payment options. For a randomly selected policyholder, let X = the number of months between successive payments. The cdf of X is as follows: 

\[F(x) = \begin{cases}
    0 & X < 1 \\
    .3 & 1 \leq X < 3 \\
    .4 & 3 \leq X < 4 \\
    .45 & 4 \leq X < 6 \\
    .6 & 6 \leq X < 12 \\
    .1 & 12 \leq X
\end{cases} \]

\textbf{a. What is the pmf of X?}

\begin{table}[h]
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\hline
x & P{[}X = x{]} \\ \hline
1 & .3 \\
3 & .1 \\
4 & .05 \\
6 & .15 \\
12 & .4
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{b.}
(i) $P(3 \leq X \leq 6) = P(6 \leq X \leq 12) - P(1 \leq X < 3) = \boxed{.3}$

(ii) $P(4 \leq X) = P(12 \leq X) - P(3 \leq X < 4) = \boxed{.6}$
