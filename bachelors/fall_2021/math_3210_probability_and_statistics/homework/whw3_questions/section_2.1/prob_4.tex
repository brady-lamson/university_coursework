\textbf{Question 4.} \newline
\hrule 

Each of a sample of four home mortgages is classified as fixed rate ($F$) or variable rate ($V$). \newline

\textbf{a. What are the 16 outcomes in $S$?} \newline
The 16 outcomes that make up our sample space are as follows: 
\begin{align*}
    S = \left\{ \begin{array}{cccc}
    FFFF, & FFFV, & FFVV, & FVVV, \\
    FVVF, & FVFF, & FVFV, & FFVF, \\
    VVVV, & VVVF, & VVFF, & VFFF, \\
    VFFV, & VFVV, & VFVF, & VVFV
    \end{array} \right\}
\end{align*} 

\textbf{b. What outcomes are in the event that exactly three of the selected mortgages are fixed rate?} \newline
The event ($A$) that satisfies these conditions has the following outcomes:
\[ A = \{ FFFV,\; FVFF,\; FFVF,\; VFFF  \}  \]

\textbf{c. Which outcomes are in the event that all four mortgages are of the same type?} \newline
The event ($B$) that satisfies these conditions has the following outcomes:
\[B = \{ FFFF,\; VVVV \}\]

\textbf{d. Which outcomes are in the event that at most one of the four is a variable-rate mortgage?} \newline
The event ($C$) that satisfies these conditions has the following outcomes:
\[C = \{ FFFF,\; FFFV,\; FVFF,\; FFVF,\; VFFF \}\]

\textbf{e. What is the union of events $B$ and $C$? What is the intersection of these two events?} \newline
The set of outcomes that are in at least one of $B$ or $C$ is as follows: 
\[B \cup C = \{ FFFF,\; FFFV,\; FVFF,\; FFVF,\; VFFF,\; VVVV\}\]
The set of outcomes that are in both $B$ and $C$ is as follows:
\[B \cap C = \{FFFF\}\]

\textbf{f. What is the union of events $A$ and $B$? What is the intersection of these two events?} \newline 
The set of outcomes that are in at least one of $A$ or $B$ is as follows:
\[A \cup B = \{ FFFV,\; FVFF,\; FFVF,\; VFFF,\; FFFF,\; VVVV\}\]

The set of outcomes that are in both $A$ and $B$ is an empty set. 
\[ A \cap B = \varnothing \]
