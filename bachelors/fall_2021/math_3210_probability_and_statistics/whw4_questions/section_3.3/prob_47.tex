\textbf{Problem 47} \\
\hrule

The article \textbf{"Should you report that fender-bender?" (\textit{Consumer Reports}), Sept. 2013:15} reported that 7 in 10 auto accidents involve a single vehicle. Suppose 15 accidents are randomly selected. Use Appendix Table A.1 to answer each of the following questions. 

\textbf{a.} What is the probability that at most 4 involve a single vehicle?

\textbf{b.} What is the probability that exactly 4 involve a single vehicle?

\textbf{c.} What is the probability that exactly 6 involve multiple vehicles?

\textbf{d.} What is the probability that between 2 and 4, inclusive, involve a single vehicle?

\textbf{e.} What is the probability that at least 2 involve a single vehicle?

\textbf{f.} What is the probability that exactly 4 involve a single vehicle and the other 11 involve multiple vehicles?
