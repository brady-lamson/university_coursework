\textbf{Prob 29.} \\
\hrule 

The pmf of the amount of memory X (GB) in a purchased flash drive was given in Example 3.13 as:

\begin{table}[h]
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\hline
x & P{[}X = x{]} \\ \hline
1 & .3 \\
3 & .1 \\
4 & .05 \\
6 & .15 \\
12 & .4
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[h]
\begin{tabular}{llll}
x & P[X = x] & $x \cdot P[X = x]$ & $(x - \mu_X)^2 \cdot P[X = x]$ \\[1mm] \cline{1-4}
1 & .05 & .05 & $(1-6.45)^2 \cdot .05 \approx 1.485$ \\[1mm]
2 & .10 & .20 & $(2-6.45)^2 \cdot .1 \approx 1.98$ \\[1mm]
4 & .35 & 1.4 & $(4-6.45)^2 \cdot .35 \approx 2.1$ \\[1mm]
8 & .40 & 3.2 & $(8-6.45)^2 \cdot .4 \approx 0.961$ \\[1mm]
16 & .10 & 1.6 & $(16-6.45)^2 \cdot .1 \approx 9.12$ \\[1mm] \cline{3-4}
 &  & $\mu_X = 6.45$ & V(X) = 15.646
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Compute the following: \newline 
\textbf{a.} $E(X) = \mu_X = \boxed{6.45}$

\textbf{b. V(X) directly from the definition} \newline 
$V(X) = \sum_{x}(x-\mu_X)^2 \cdot P[X = x] = \boxed{15.646}$

\textbf{c. The standard deviation of X} \newline 
$\sigma = \sqrt{V(X)} = \sqrt{15.646} \approx \boxed{3.96}$

\textbf{d. V(X) using the shortcut formula} \newline 
$V(X) = E(X^2) - [E(X)]^2 = 57.25 - 6.45^2 = \boxed{15.648}$
