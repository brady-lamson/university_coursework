\textbf{Question 39.} \\
\hrule 

A box in a supply room contains 15 compact fluorescent lightbulbs, of which 5 are rated 13-watt, 6 are rated 18-watt, and 4 are rated 23-watt. Suppose that three of these bulbs are randomly selected.

\textbf{a. What is the probability that exactly two of the selected bulbs are rated 23-watt?}
\[P(E) = \frac{n(E)}{n(S)} = \frac{\binom{4}{2} \cdot \binom{11}{1}}{\binom{15}{3}} \approx \boxed{.145}\]

\textbf{b. What is the probability that all three of the bulbs are the same rank?} \newline 
Our three possibilities are (All A), (All B) and (All C). So we want the sum of the probabilities of these three possibilities.
\begin{align*}
    P(All A) + (All B) + (All C) \\
    \frac{\binom{5}{3}}{\binom{15}{3}} + \frac{\binom{6}{3}}{\binom{15}{3}} + \frac{\binom{4}{3}}{\binom{15}{3}} \approx \boxed{0.0747} 
\end{align*}
