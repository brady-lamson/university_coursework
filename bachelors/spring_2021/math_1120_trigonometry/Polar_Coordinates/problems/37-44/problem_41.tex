\begin{problem}[41]
    (-3,0)
\end{problem}

\begin{solution}
    We can use the exact same tools to solve for different things here. It's fairly straightforward so let's get to it. \medskip
    
    Calculating $r$: 
    \begin{align}
        r^2 &= x^2 + y^2 \\
        r^2 &= -3^2 + 0^2 \\
        r &= 3
    \end{align}
    
    Calculating $\theta$ part 1:
    \begin{align}
        x &= r \cdot \cos(\theta) \\
        \theta &= \arccos \left( \frac{x}{r} \right) \\
        \theta &= \arccos \left( \frac{-3}{3} \right) \\
        \theta &= \pi 
        \end{align}
    
    Calculating $\theta$ part 2:
    \begin{align}
        y &= r \cdot \sin(\theta) \\
        \theta &= \arcsin \left( \frac{y}{r} \right) \\
        \theta &= \arccos \left( \frac{0}{3} \right) \\
        \theta &= 0, \pi, 2\pi
    \end{align}
    
    \[\boxed{ \left( 3, \pi \right)}\]
\end{solution}
