\begin{problem}[3.]
    $z = 6i$
\end{problem}

\begin{solution}
    Please refer to problem 1 for more detailed logic. 
    
    \[z = a + bi\]
    \[a = 0 \; \text{and} \; b = 6 \]
    \[ \boxed{0 = Re(z)} \; \text{and} \; \boxed{ 6 = Im(z) }\]
    
    \textbf{Theorem 3:} Since $Re(z) = 0$ and $Im(z) > 0$ then $\boxed{arg(z) = \left\{ \frac{\pi}{2} + 2\pi k | \; \text{k is an integer} \right\} }$
    
    From here we can determine $Arg(z)$ as well, which is $\boxed{\frac{\pi}{2}}$. 
    
    Calculating $r$.
    \begin{align}
        a^2 + b^2 = r^2 \\
        0^2 + 6^2 = r^2 \\
        \boxed{r = 6 = |z|}
    \end{align}
    
    Last is our polar representation. 
    \[\boxed{ \text{Polar Rep.} \; = 6 \; cis \left( \frac{\pi}{2} \right) }\]
\end{solution}
